Listed pharma & healthcare stocks

Listed pharma and healthcare companies in four markets: A-share, Hong Kong, United States and Switzerland. Pick a market, click a column header to sort, and use the row under the headers to filter any column. All amounts are in the listing currency.

Number filters: >100, <0, 10-50. Percent columns take plain numbers (>20 means above 20%). Text columns match any keyword.

Notes and sources

S² Capital operates a simulated portfolio using real market prices. No actual capital is deployed, and nothing on this site is investment advice.
